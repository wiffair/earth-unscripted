# Earth Unscripted licence scope

The root MIT licence applies to original program code, shaders, native wrappers,
build/data-processing scripts and original technical documentation, to the extent
Richard Cole and the contributors hold those rights. Third-party headers and
licence notices are preserved. Renaming the project does not revoke earlier MIT
permissions.

Original icon and splash artwork in `Brand/` and
`Unity/Assets/Resources/EarthUnscripted/`, including the owner's contribution to
AI-assisted artwork, are offered under **CC BY-SA 4.0** where copyright applies.
Their generation prompts and provenance accompany the images. No trademark
endorsement is granted by this copyright licence.

The owner's supplied music and station recordings in the separate radio release
have their own **CC BY-SA 4.0** scope and per-file attribution. Fictional artist,
song, station and presenter names remain intact. Source recordings, edited
metadata and importer-compatible archives are identified separately; changing
tags does not silently change an existing pack revision or approval hash.

The reviewed VW Splittie model/livery retains its existing MIT grant and is also
offered under CC BY-SA 4.0 for original contributor artwork. Its modelling scripts
use MIT and its engine recordings retain CC BY 4.0 / CC0. See
[the camper review](ContentSources/VW-Splittie/PROVENANCE.md) for exact scope, hashes
and brand identifiers.

Excluded from both blanket grants:

- Unity Editor, engine/player runtime, package caches, fonts and other Unity
  components. Install Unity and resolve the pinned packages under their terms.
- OSM/Geofabrik databases and derivatives (ODbL 1.0), OGL-supplied data and
  Copernicus DEM data. Retain their respective notices and dataset obligations.
- Car Concept appearance (CC BY 4.0), Poly Haven/ambientCG resources (CC0),
  Clipper2 (BSL-1.0), SDL and its embedded components, SQLite, Newtonsoft and
  NLayer (MIT), and other separately attributed dependencies. Their own terms apply.
  NLayer source pins, local import bounds and its upstream licence are in
  `Provenance/NLayer/`.
- Unlicensed reference media, real-brand vehicle packs awaiting a separate
  provenance review, private development archives, personal files and saves.

The repository does not contain Unity itself and does not make the proprietary
engine open source. It makes the project's original source available under MIT.
See `Provenance/` and `Docs/RELEASE_STATUS.md` for exact inclusion and limitations.
