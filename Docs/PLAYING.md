# Getting started

Download the [Beta 52 game](https://github.com/wiffair/earth-unscripted/releases/tag/v0.23.0-beta.52)
for your computer, extract the ZIP and open Earth Unscripted.

## Mac

Choose **EarthUnscripted-0.23.0-beta.52-macOS-arm64.zip** for an Apple silicon Mac
(M1 or newer) running **macOS 15 or newer**. Open **Earth Unscripted Beta.app**, or
drag the app into Applications first.

macOS may ask you to approve the beta in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
Check that your download came from this project's release before approving it.

## Windows

Choose **EarthUnscripted-0.23.0-beta.52-Windows-x64.zip** for 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.
Use **Extract All**, then open **Earth Unscripted.exe** in the extracted folder.
Keep the launcher and its `Players` folders together.

Windows may show a reputation warning because the beta has no publisher signing
certificate.

## Standard or HD?

| Choice | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **Standard** | Better performance and lighter graphics workloads |
| **HD** | Richer lighting and reflections on more capable hardware |

Both choices share your progress. Start with Standard if you are unsure.
Save and close the current drive before switching versions.

## Your first drive

Choose a location, prepare the area, then select a road to start driving. New
areas need internet access and may take longer to prepare on the first visit.
The game keeps visited areas in its adjustable caches.

Click the small map to pause, browse the larger map and choose a destination.
Follow the route on the driving map, or save a location to return to later.
The game also remembers where you finished your last drive.

Settings include controller feedback, audio, graphics and cache sizes. Beta
progress is saved separately from the original live game.

## Scenery and music downloads

The Radio-Music ZIP is an optional MP3 collection for listening outside the game.
SceneryCore, RadioRuntime and ValidationFixtures are for developers
[building from source](BUILDING.md).
