01 / The game

The world outside.
A journey of your own.

Earth Unscripted turns real roads and terrain into a place to explore.

Pick somewhere familiar or somewhere you’ve never been. Follow the sat nav, take the next side road, or head out with no destination at all. Scenery builds around you as you travel.

Traffic, changing weather and the passing daylight give the same road a different feel each time you return.

No two journeys quite the same.Route illustration

Find your own way

There’s more to a road
than getting there.

Set a destination on the map and follow the route as you drive. Or ignore the next turning and find something else.

Save the places you want to come back to. Your next drive picks up where you left off.

Opening the beta for the first time

Mac: Extract the ZIP and open Earth Unscripted Beta.app. This beta is ad-hoc signed, so macOS may ask you to approve it in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Windows: Use Extract All, then open Earth Unscripted.exe. Keep the Players folders beside the launcher. Windows may show a publisher reputation warning.

Choose a location, prepare the area and select a road. Internet access is needed to fetch new areas; visited places use the game’s adjustable caches.
