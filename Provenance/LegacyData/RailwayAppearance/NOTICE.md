# Railway appearance sources

Stage 0.17 reuses existing reviewed local textures. No new textures were downloaded, no new third-party assets were acquired, and this change creates no new third-party licence category. Exact source URLs, resource byte hashes, runtime tints/repeats and source-manifest links are in `manifest.json`.

- **Masonry:** Poly Haven `rock_wall_03`, photography by Charlotte Baglioni, processing by Dario Barresi, under **CC0-1.0**. [Source](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_wall_03). Existing reviewed colour, OpenGL normal and derived metallic/smoothness resources are reused from `Data/BuildingAppearance`. Its documented tile size is **2.57 × 2.57 metres**. This is fictional masonry selection, not a survey or claim about the real bridge's construction material.
- **Ballast approximation:** Poly Haven `rock_boulder_cracked`, **CC0-1.0**, reused from `Data/Art/texture-manifest.json`. [Source](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_cracked). The deliberate 2.7 repeats/metre art scale approximates coarse ballast; this is not a dedicated gravel photograph or measured ballast size. The abandoned variant changes tint only.
- **Approach-earth approximation:** Poly Haven `aerial_grass_rock`, **CC0-1.0**, reused from the same existing art manifest. [Source](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock). Brown tint and 0.20 repeats/metre are fictional art choices, not measured soil.
- **Sleepers:** original project-generated 128 × 128 grayscale grain, seeded `System.Random(71023)`, tinted brown by the material. The implementation is `RailwayMaterials.Sleepers`; no wood/bark photograph is used. The generated texture is shared and uploaded once. No separate licence has been selected for original project code/art; CC0 applies to the identified external sources, not automatically to the project implementation.

[Poly Haven licence](https://polyhaven.com/license), [CC0 public-domain dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Existing source-review records and original notices remain authoritative; this notice records their additional railway use. Packed masonry smoothness is the existing reviewed derivative of the roughness map, with dielectric metallic RGB0 and smoothness alpha `255 − roughness`.

Rail and barrier metal remain original colour/roughness material choices. UVs are in world metres: horizontal XZ projection and vertical global-height courses. Recalculated tangents enable normal mapping. Geometry, source elevations and collisions are unchanged by the appearance selection. Native images require visual review before making any realism claim.
